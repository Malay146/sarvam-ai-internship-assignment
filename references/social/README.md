## What this tells the banner recipes

1. **Five layouts cover almost everything:**
   - **Gateway window** (02, 06, 09)
   - **Nested gateway echoes** (12)
   - **Soft cloud** (05, 07)
   - **Split text + glossy shapes** (03, 10)
   - **Shape cluster** (11)
2. **Wordmark** is small, either top-centre (stacked above the headline) or top-left. Partner lockups use the dot separator (`sarvam · SUPERNOVA`) or a product name after a space (`sarvam Kaze`).
3. **Headlines** are short (2–7 words), sentence or title case, centred in symmetric layouts and left-aligned in splits.
   - **Season Mix** for stories and programs.
   - **Matter semibold** for launches and research titles.
   - Never more than one headline and one sub-line.
4. **Gradients** go well beyond the website's blue-to-orange: olive/gold, green/periwinkle, saffron/magenta, cream/violet. Always smooth, grainy and vertical, with a lighter "glow" area behind the text.
5. **Shapes** are our studio families: cusped gateways, scalloped medallions, flowers. They appear as glossy 3D-ish fills (gradient + inner light) or as nested outlines (echo rings, as in 12).
6. **Lots of air.** The headline takes about 30–45% of the width. Margins are about 6–8% of the short side.
7. **Same art on every platform:** we design each banner once and adapt the frame (1080×1080, 1080×1350, 1200×627) by rearranging, not cropping blindly.
8. **One CTA at most**, as a small chip ("Apply Now"). Most posts have none.

## Where our system goes further

- Sibling consistency and exact type roles (Sarvam mixes Matter semibold and Season Mix inconsistently across launches).
- The contrast rule for symbols (e.g. the gold-gradient numbers in 09 are low-contrast on a pale ground).
- One CTA style, used consistently.
